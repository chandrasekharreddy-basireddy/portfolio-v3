A bit about me

I'm Chandra — a computer science student who learns by building things I'm probably not qualified to build yet. It works out more often than it should.

Survival School started as a way to make exam practice less boring and ended up teaching me databases, auth and deployment the hard way. Signal-Lite began as a simple question — how do chat apps actually work? — and turned into an education in security I'm still working through.

What I care about most is software that respects the person using it: fast, honest, and it doesn't break when the wifi does. That's the bar I hold my own work to — and when it doesn't clear it, I fix it.

Outside of code, I'm usually somewhere between a DSA problem set and a Power BI dashboard, automating something with n8n so I never have to do it manually again. C taught me how computers actually think; Python is where I think out loud.
